Translations: bs

Aereogramme - No Really, Everything's Fine

The song is in C# minor, but it's using a lot of non-diatonic notes. I'm not sure if that's something inherent to the song or a mistake introduced by me while transcribing it. Already in bar 4: there is a D. Bar 8 has an F-A# perfect fourth, but I'm not quite sure about that. It sounds like they might be playing a harmonic. There is a C in bar 123, but I'm pretty sure it's correct, since it's giving us the most characteristic chord of this song: the major7sus4. If someone has more insight about this, please share it in the comments.

The piano ist once again the weakest link. At first I tried to transcribe it myself, but I didn't quite like a few spots. I used the Prism audio to MIDI converter to try and fix it, but I was disappointed with the result. It did help me fix a few spots: the descending lines in bar 10 and 11. The calm bridge starting from bar 121 is an approximation, there's a lot of intricate, gentle playing there, but the gist should be there.

I assume that Aereogramme play this in Drop C#, but I'm too lazy to retune constantly and play it in Drop C and adjusting the fingering. The tab is written in Drop C#.
